Chapter 1

Historiography: Development in the West 📜

This chapter explores the development of historiography in the Western world, covering key thinkers and their contributions to historical writing. It introduces various schools of thought including dialectics, Marxist, Subaltern, and Feminist historiography.

Key Concepts:
  • Identify the founders and key contributors to modern historiography.
  • Understand different schools of historical thought.
  • Explain the contributions of Georg Hegel, Karl Marx, Leopold von Ranke, and Simone de Beauvoir.
  • Recognize the different types of historiography like Subaltern and Feminist.
  • Understand the concept of dialectics and class struggle.

Chapter 2

Historiography: Indian Tradition 🇮🇳

This chapter examines the Indian tradition of historiography, focusing on historians who wrote about India's past. It covers the works of Indian and British historians who documented Indian history.

Key Concepts:
  • Identify key Indian historians and their works.
  • Understand the contributions of British historians to Indian historiography.
  • Recognize the importance of different historical sources.
  • Appreciate the diverse perspectives in Indian historical writing.

Chapter 3

Applied History 🏛️

This chapter explores the practical application of historical knowledge in fields like heritage management, tourism, and conservation. It discusses the importance of preserving and conserving historical artifacts and sites for future generations.

Key Concepts:
  • Understand the concept of applied history.
  • Explain the importance of archives and museums.
  • Identify methods of preserving and conserving heritage.
  • Recognize the role of UNESCO in heritage management.
  • Understand the need for trained professionals in conservation.

Mass Media and History 📺

This chapter explores the relationship between mass media and history, discussing how newspapers, radio, and television have evolved and influenced society. It covers the role of media in democracy and its importance as a historical source.
