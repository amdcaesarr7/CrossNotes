Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Welcome to CrossNotes (accessible at https://crossnotes.rf.gd/). Your privacy is important to us, especially because our platform is created for students preparing for their Maharashtra State Board Class 10 examinations.

This Privacy Policy clearly outlines what information is collected, how it is used, and how your privacy is safeguarded.

1. Information We Collect

CrossNotes is designed to be privacy-first. When browsing our website:

  • No Mandatory Personal Information: You can browse all public notes, curriculum details, and features without registering or sharing personal details.
  • Local Device Storage: We use browser localStorage purely to remember your preferences (such as your chosen Light/Dark theme mode) and local app states. This information never leaves your browser.
  • Optional App Authentication: If you choose to log in to the interactive CrossNotes web app to save your study streaks, earn XP, and participate in the community leaderboard, authentication is securely handled via Google OAuth / Firebase. We only receive your public display name, email address, and profile avatar provided by Google.

2. Cookies and Tracking

CrossNotes does not use invasive advertising trackers or sell your personal data. We do not place advertising cookies. Third-party content delivery networks (like Google Fonts) may log standard request information (such as your IP address and user agent) to deliver font files securely to your browser.

3. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted third-party providers to power our platform:

  • Google Fonts: Provides typography to render study notes cleanly.
  • Firebase (Google LLC): Provides authentication and cloud database services for students who save their XP and study streaks.
  • Vercel & InfinityFree: Web hosting and edge content delivery infrastructure.

4. Student and Child Privacy

CrossNotes is built for Class 10 secondary students. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without parental consent. Parents or legal guardians with questions about their student's usage can contact us anytime.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact the maintainer directly at:
