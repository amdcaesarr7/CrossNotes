Chapter 1

Historiography: Development in the West 📜

This chapter traces the development of historiography in the West, from early Greek and Roman historians through medieval, Renaissance, and Enlightenment thinkers, to modern scientific historiography with figures like Ranke, Marx, and Annales school historians.

Key Concepts to Master:
  • Define historiography and its scope.
  • Trace the development of historical writing from ancient Greece to modern times.
  • Understand contributions of Voltaire, Ranke, Marx, and the Annales school.
  • Explain key concepts like dialectics, materialism, and feminist historiography.
  • Identify the four characteristics of modern historiography.

Chapter 2

Historiography: Indian Tradition 📚

This chapter covers the Indian tradition of historiography, from ancient texts like the Vedas and Puranas through medieval chronicles, colonial-era writings, and the development of nationalist, Marxist, and subaltern historiography in modern India.

Key Concepts to Master:
  • Describe ancient Indian historical traditions including Vedic, Puranic, and Buddhist sources.
  • Understand medieval Indian historiography including Persian and Sanskrit works.
  • Trace the development of colonial and nationalist historiography.
  • Identify key Indian historians and their contributions.
  • Explain subaltern and feminist historiography in India.

  • Explain the role of history in heritage management.
  • Understand the importance of museums and archives.
  • Describe the relationship between history and tourism.

Chapter 4

History of Indian Arts 🎨

This chapter traces the history of Indian art forms — architecture, sculpture, painting, music, and dance — from ancient times through the medieval and modern periods. It covers temple architecture styles (Nagara, Dravid, Hemadpanti), cave paintings, classical and folk music traditions, and classical dance forms.

Key Concepts to Master:
  • Identify the main styles of Indian temple architecture.
  • Describe the development of Indian painting traditions.
  • Distinguish between classical and folk music and dance forms.
  • Understand the contributions of key artists and patrons to Indian art.
  • Explain the six main branches of Indian classical painting.
